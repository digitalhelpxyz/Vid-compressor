Vid Compressor

Fast, Offline & Secure Video Compression for Android

📱 About Vid Compressor

Vid Compressor is a powerful Android app designed to reduce video file size without compromising quality. It works completely offline and does not upload your videos to any server.

🚀 Key Features

  • Offline video compression
  • Multiple resolution options (Original, 1080p, 720p, 480p)
  • Audio keep or remove option
  • Custom output file name
  • Fast and smooth compression process
  • No watermark

🔐 Privacy & Security

Your privacy is our priority. All video processing happens locally on your device. Vid Compressor does not collect, store, or share personal data.

📊 Compression Details

After compression, the app shows detailed information including original size, compressed size, resolution, duration, and space saved.

📥 Download & Use

Vid Compressor is optimized for Android 12 and above. Perfect for saving storage space and sharing videos faster on social media.
